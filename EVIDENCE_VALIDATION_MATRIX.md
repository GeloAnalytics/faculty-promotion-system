# Documentary Evidence Validation Matrix

This document is a working, repo-level summary of the evidence requirements reflected in `Reference.xlsx` and the current upload workflow.

The rule is intentionally strict:

- `AND` means every listed item is required.
- `OR` means any one valid alternative may satisfy that slot.
- If a required item in an `AND` bundle is missing, the submission stays incomplete.
- If an `OR` branch is used, at least one qualifying document must be present for that branch.
- Optional bonus criteria do not replace required criteria.

## Packet-Level Requirements

A promotion packet should not be treated as complete unless it includes:

1. The Request Form
2. The Individual Summary Sheet
3. The checklist of evidence submitted
4. The documentary evidence copies or drive-access links used for validation
5. A consistent identity match across the request form, ISS, and supporting evidence

If any of these are missing, the packet should stay in incomplete status.

## KRA I - Instruction

### Criterion A - Teaching Effectiveness

Required evidence includes the faculty performance record used for the score sheet.

### Criterion B - Curriculum and/or Instructional Materials Developed

This criterion uses an `AND/OR` style bundle:

- curriculum development evidence
- and/or instructional materials evidence

At least one valid branch must be complete, and any branch that is selected must include its supporting documents.

### Criterion C - Thesis, Dissertation, and Mentorship Services

Required evidence includes the supporting service documents for thesis, dissertation, capstone, special project, or mentorship work.

## KRA II - Research, Innovation, and Creative Work

### Criterion A - Research Outputs Published

Required evidence includes published scholarly outputs and their publication proof.

### Criterion B - Inventions

This criterion may be satisfied by one or more of these evidence paths:

- patentable inventions
- utility models
- industrial designs
- copyrighted and utilized software products
- new plant variety, animal breed, or microbial strain evidence

### Criterion C - Creative Works

This criterion may be satisfied by one or more of these evidence paths:

- creative performing artwork
- exhibition
- juried or peer-reviewed design
- literary publication

## KRA III - Extension Services

### Criterion A - Service to the Institution

Required evidence includes proof of the institutional service activity claimed.

### Criterion B - Service to the Community

Required evidence includes the community service, consultancy, outreach, or related engagement documents claimed by the faculty member.

### Criterion C - Quality of Extension Service

Required evidence includes the client satisfaction or extension-quality documentation used for the score.

### Criterion D - Bonus Criterion

This is a bonus path. It does not replace the required KRA III criteria.

## KRA IV - Professional Development

### Criterion A - Involvement in Professional Organization

Required evidence includes current membership and active-role or contribution proof.

### Criterion B - Continuing Development

Required evidence includes the continuing-development artifacts used for the score, such as conferences, training, or related professional development documents.

### Criterion C - Awards and Recognition

Required evidence includes the award or recognition proof.

### Criterion D - Bonus Criterion

This is a bonus path. It does not replace the required KRA IV criteria.

## Validation Interpretation

The safe working interpretation for the app is:

1. Every required criterion must have evidence.
2. An `OR` clause allows alternatives, but not omission.
3. Missing evidence should be surfaced as an incomplete validation state, not as a final promotable result.
4. Final evaluator rules can tighten this matrix further, but they should not relax the requirement that required evidence be present.

## Status

This matrix is aligned with the uploaded workbook structure and the current upload workflow. It should be revised again once the evaluator-provided document is finalized.
